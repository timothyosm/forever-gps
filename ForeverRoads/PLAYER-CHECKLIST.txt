Forever GPS 2.1.17 — manual in-game check

These checks are performed by you. The automated tests run outside WoW.

1. Start WoW normally, or type /reload if it is already open.
   Type /froads. Scroll the zone list: it should include the original zones,
   all six capitals, Mount Hyjal, Riverglades, Shen'dralas, Zephras Isle,
   Darkspear Islands and the three battlegrounds. Zephras has two map IDs.

2. Place a native map pin on nearby reachable ground. You can select an
   existing pin or type /froads pin to route to it. Move the pin and
   confirm the route updates. Select an active quest marker and confirm the
   route switches to that quest. Removing an active pin clears its route.
   Once the search finishes, look for a gold dotted trail on the world
   map and its nearby portion on the minimap. Look for the travel estimate
   and steps in the world map’s upper-left corner. Walk along a short
   section and check that the estimate decreases.
   For a longer trip, look for “Planning route…” until the itinerary is
   ready. The first itinerary should already include any selected rides.
   On a route with a ride, zoom between the zone, continent, and Azeroth
   maps to check that light-blue dots mark the connection.
   Match each transport icon to its arrival stop and the gold boot to the
   end of the run on the world map. On the minimap, look for flight, boat
   and zeppelin arrival icons alongside the dotted trail.
   If you are in Stonetalon, also try:
   /froads way stonetalon 46.2 58.5 Sun Rock Retreat

3. Close and reopen the map, and try a different nearby destination.
   Check that guidance stays responsive and that the old route is replaced.
   /froads pause should hide guidance; /froads resume should restore it.
   Left-click the minimap button to open settings; right-click pauses/resumes.

4. Run /froads perf with the map open, then again after closing it.
   It prints map drawing, terrain search and transport timings separately.
   Try the same distant quest or pin twice. Check the itinerary elapsed
   time and cache counters in /froads perf.
   After a route is found and you stand still, redraw counts should stay
   steady. Occasional peaks do not describe sustained FPS; also note whether
   the game feels smooth while walking and choosing a new destination.

5. If something fails, report:
   - Your zone, approximate map coordinates and destination.
   - Whether the world map, minimap or both are affected.
   - For lag, use /froads perf start, repeat the slow action, then use
     /froads perf stop and /froads perf log. Share the resulting log text.
   - Any visible error message, and whether pausing guidance changes the lag.

Use /froads follow to return to the tracked quest after testing.

Optional while playing normally: open a flight master and check the learned
flight stop count in /froads. Set a distant destination and use /froads status
to see any flights and crossings. Consecutive flights appear as one step
and one dotted connector to the final flight master. Select flights and board boats, zeppelins
and the tram manually. While flying, select a quest near the landing and
check for the onward trail and an “after landing” estimate on the world map.
Check that walking guidance resumes from your position when you land.
For a boat or zeppelin in the itinerary, open the destination zone map
during the crossing and check for the trail from the exit and an
“after docking” estimate.
The maps draw the current walking leg. Battleground and introductory-island
entry uses the game's queue or entrance.

Offline evidence: all 55 native zone-map IDs are included, every packed
terrain block decompresses, and sampled routes render within all 55 maps in
the Lua test harness. This does not claim that all locations were walked
or FPS was measured in the live client. Your results complete the live check.
